# xTool P2S — Laser-Cut Mathematics Manipulatives for K–12 STEM

> An **independent project proposal** to build a reusable library of laser-cut mathematics manipulatives for a K–12 STEM math lab. Designed to be cut and engraved on the **xTool P2S** (portable CO₂ laser cutter).

**Core idea:** Turn abstract mathematical ideas into physical objects students can **move, compare, assemble, measure, and test** — so every manipulative helps a student *answer a mathematical question*, not just look attractive.

## Why a laser cutter (and why the xTool P2S)

- **Abstract → physical:** students manipulate a rule, a graph, a unit circle, or an algebra tile with their hands.
- **Reusable & standardized:** one base board + interchangeable pieces + a teacher guide per topic.
- **Portable production:** the P2S is portable, cuts/engraves plywood, MDF, approved acrylic, and cardstock, and its enclosed design + camera alignment + approved-material guidance suit supervised school production.
- **Safety first:** never cut PVC or unknown plastics; verify material composition and the manufacturer's approved-material list; use ventilation, fire precautions, and trained adult supervision.

## Design → Cut → Report loop

```
1. LEARNING OBJECTIVE  →  what will the student explain or do?
2. WHAT TO MANIPULATE  →  the physical motion must show the math relationship
3. DESIGN THE PARTS    →  account for thickness, kerf, slots, tolerances
4. CUT & TEST          →  fit, durability, readability, independence
5. PAIR WITH CHALLENGE →  question + prediction + investigation + reflection
6. EVALUATE & REPORT   →  Evaluation_Sheet.md + Survey_Sheet.md, by grade level
```
