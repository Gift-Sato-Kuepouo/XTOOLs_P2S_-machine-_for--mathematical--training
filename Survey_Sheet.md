# Survey Sheet
## Laser-Cut Mathematics Manipulatives — Student & Teacher Surveys

 Two audiences, three moments. Use **Pre** (before using the manipulative), **Post** (right after), and **Teacher/Supervisor** (end of session or unit). Identical pre/post wording makes the gain measurable.

### Student Survey — scoring
- **Confidence gain** = mean Post (items 1–3) − mean Pre (items 1–3). Target: **≥ +0.5** on the 5-point scale.
- **Usability score** = mean of items 7–10 (Post). Target: **≥ 4.0**.
- **Grade-level note:** For K–2, read aloud and use the 3-emoji version (😟 / 😐 / 😊). For 9–12, add a written 2-sentence justification for item 3.
